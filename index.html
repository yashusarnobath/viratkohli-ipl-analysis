01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

UNDERSTANDING THE CAREER

This project analyses Virat Kohli's IPL batting career using ball-by-ball data. Rather than looking only at career totals, the analysis examines how his runs were constructed, how often he found boundaries, how dot-ball pressure changed, how his strike rate varied against different bowling types and how his scoring intensity changed across innings phases.

KEY QUESTIONS

How did Kohli's run profile change across IPL seasons?

Did his scoring efficiency change over time?

How did his strike rate vary against pace and spin?

How did his scoring intensity vary across batting phases?

What dismissal patterns appeared in his IPL career?

02 · RUN-SCORING EVOLUTION

HOW DID THE RUN PROFILE CHANGE?

This analysis breaks Kohli's seasonal run production into 1s, 2s, 3s, fours and sixes while tracking strike rate. The purpose is to understand not only how many runs he scored, but how those runs were constructed.

QUESTION

Was Kohli's scoring output driven primarily by accumulation, boundary hitting, or a combination of both across different seasons?

WHAT THE TABLEAU SHOWS

The dashboard shows substantial variation in Kohli's run production and strike rate across the IPL seasons.

The most prominent run-scoring peak in the displayed data is 2016, when Kohli scored 973 runs at a 152.03 strike rate.

The 2016 run composition shows 409 runs from 1s, 2s and 3s, 336 runs from fours and 228 runs from sixes.

The later seasons also show high scoring intensity, with the dashboard recording a 154.70 strike rate in 2024 and 165.85 in 2026.

The chart therefore shows that Kohli's run production has come through a combination of running between the wickets and boundary scoring, with the balance between these components changing across seasons.

03 · SCORING EFFICIENCY

DID KOHLI BECOME A MORE EFFICIENT SCORER?

This analysis tracks balls per boundary and dot-ball percentage across IPL seasons. Together, these measures provide a view of how frequently Kohli found boundaries and how often he faced deliveries without scoring.

QUESTION

Did Kohli's scoring efficiency change across seasons through boundary frequency and dot-ball pressure?

WHAT THE TABLEAU SHOWS

The dashboard shows considerable variation in both balls per boundary and dot-ball percentage across seasons.

In 2022, Kohli recorded 7.35 balls per boundary and a 39.12% dot-ball rate in the displayed data.

In 2024, balls per boundary fell to 4.79, while the dot-ball rate was 30.90%.

By 2026, balls per boundary had fallen further to 4.15, while the dot-ball rate was 26.11%.

The displayed later-season data therefore shows boundaries occurring more frequently alongside a lower proportion of dot balls compared with 2022.

The matchup dashboard shows that Kohli's strike rate varies substantially across individual bowling styles.

For example, in the displayed 2024 data, the strike rate reaches 242.9 against Medium Fast, while the displayed values for Fast, Fast Medium and several spin categories are lower.

The dashboard also shows differences within spin bowling. For example, the displayed 2024 value against Off Spin is 127.2.

In 2026, the displayed values again vary by bowling style, with Medium Fast at 186.7, Fast Medium at 185.5 and Fast at 172.3.

The result shows why an overall Pace-versus-Spin comparison can hide important differences between individual bowling styles.

WHAT THE TABLEAU SHOWS

The dashboard shows clear differences in Kohli's strike rate between the Powerplay, Middle Overs and Death Overs.

The Death Overs contain some of the highest displayed seasonal strike rates in the chart, reaching 266.7 in 2015 and 266.7 in 2019.

The displayed Death Overs strike rate is also 216.0 in 2023 and 237.5 in 2026.

The dashboard therefore shows that Kohli's scoring intensity is not uniform throughout an innings and changes according to the batting phase.

Very high phase-specific strike rates should be interpreted alongside balls faced, because smaller samples can produce large seasonal variations.

07 · ANALYSIS SUMMARY

WHAT DOES THE DATA SHOW?

The individual Tableau analyses provide different views of Kohli's IPL batting profile. Together, they show how his run production, scoring efficiency, bowling matchups, batting phases and dismissal patterns vary across the data.

KEY FINDINGS FROM THE TABLEAU ANALYSIS

Run production: 2016 stands out with 973 runs at a 152.03 strike rate, while the run-composition chart shows substantial contributions from both accumulation and boundaries.

Scoring efficiency: The displayed later seasons show a fall in balls per boundary and dot-ball percentage, reaching 4.15 balls per boundary and 26.11% dot balls in 2026.

Bowling matchups: Strike rate varies considerably across individual bowling styles, demonstrating why Pace versus Spin alone does not capture the complete matchup picture.

Batting phases: Kohli's scoring intensity varies across Powerplay, Middle Overs and Death Overs, with some of the highest displayed seasonal strike rates occurring in the Death Overs.

Dismissals: Caught dismissals are the dominant dismissal category in the displayed data, followed by bowled and LBW dismissals.

Together, these dashboards provide a multi-dimensional view of Kohli's IPL batting — not only how many runs he scored, but how those runs were constructed, how efficiently he scored, which bowling profiles he faced, when he accelerated and how his innings ended.
