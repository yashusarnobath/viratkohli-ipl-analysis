01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

UNDERSTANDING THE CAREER

This project analyses Virat Kohli's IPL batting career using ball-by-ball data. Instead of looking only at total runs, the analysis examines how those runs were constructed, how efficiently he scored, how his performance varied against different bowling types and how his scoring changed across different phases of an innings.

KEY QUESTIONS

How did Kohli's scoring profile change across seasons?
How did his scoring efficiency change?
How did his strike rate vary against pace and spin?
How did his scoring intensity change across batting phases?
What dismissal patterns appeared throughout his career?

07 · PERFORMANCE STORY

FROM PEAK TO REVIVAL

Bringing the analysis together, this section connects Kohli's run production, scoring efficiency, bowling matchups, batting phases and dismissal patterns to understand the broader evolution of his IPL batting.

QUESTION

What changed in Kohli's batting profile across the different phases of his IPL career?

PEAK

2016 stands out as a defining run-scoring season in the analysis. Kohli scored 973 runs at a 152.03 strike rate, combining exceptional run volume with a high scoring rate. The run-composition view also shows a substantial contribution from both accumulation and boundary scoring.

DIP

2022 provides a contrasting scoring-efficiency profile. The dashboard records 7.35 balls per boundary and a 39.12% dot-ball rate, indicating a lower boundary frequency and higher dot-ball percentage than the later seasons shown in the analysis.

REVIVAL

The later-period data shows increased scoring intensity alongside improved scoring efficiency. Balls per boundary fell to 4.79 in 2024 and 4.15 in 2026, while dot-ball percentage fell to 30.90% in 2024 and 26.11% in 2026. Strike rate also reached 154.70 in 2024 and 165.85 in 2026 in the run-scoring dashboard.

FINAL ANALYST NOTE

The evolution was not simply about how many runs Kohli scored — it was about how those runs were constructed. Across the dashboards, the story can be viewed through run volume, boundary contribution, dot-ball pressure, bowling matchups, innings phases and dismissal patterns. Together, these dimensions provide a more complete view of Kohli's IPL batting evolution than career totals alone.
