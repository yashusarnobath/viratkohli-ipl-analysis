01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

UNDERSTANDING THE CAREER

This project analyses Virat Kohli's IPL batting career using ball-by-ball data. Instead of looking only at total runs, the analysis examines how those runs were constructed, how efficiently he scored and how his performance changed against different bowling types.

KEY QUESTIONS
How did Kohli's scoring profile change across seasons? How did his strike rate vary against pace and spin? Did boundary frequency and dot-ball pressure change? What dismissal patterns appeared throughout his career?
02 · RUN-SCORING EVOLUTION

HOW DID THE RUN PROFILE CHANGE?

This analysis breaks Kohli's seasonal run production into 1s, 2s, 3s, fours and sixes while tracking strike rate. The objective is to understand not just how many runs he scored, but how those runs were created.

QUESTION
Was Kohli's scoring output driven primarily by accumulation, boundary hitting, or a combination of both?

06 · PERFORMANCE STORY

PEAK → DIP → REVIVAL

The final stage of the project connects the individual analyses into one performance story. The aim is to explain what changed across different phases of Kohli's IPL career using measurable indicators.

PEAK

Identify the period where the selected performance indicators reached their strongest levels and explain what the data shows.

DIP

Identify the period where important performance indicators declined and explain which components changed.

REVIVAL

Identify the later period where performance indicators improved and explain the measurable changes.
